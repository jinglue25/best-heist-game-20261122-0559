# VAULTBREAK: The Meridian Job

Become Wren, a master thief slipping into the Meridian Exchange after dark. Cross three hand-designed floors of watchmen, sweeping cameras and glittering prizes, from the Gallery Wing to the Counting House and finally the Meridian Vault. Brass, stone, quiet footsteps and one impossible score: every asset is original canvas art and every sound is synthesized locally.

Move with **WASD or arrows**. Hold **Shift** to sneak and **Space** near a treasure to crack it. **Click** to toss a limited decoy coin toward the pointer; nearby guards investigate. Press **E** at a brass camera terminal for a ten-second blackout. **P** pauses, **R** restarts the current job, and **Enter/Space** starts or advances screens. Walk into the west getaway van to leave at any time.

Guard cones stop at walls and furniture; cameras see over furniture. Moving fast, standing close and lamplight raise suspicion faster. Hide to let the alarm decay. At 100%, guards converge and a twenty-second lockdown begins: escape before it closes, and do not let a guard touch you. Red lasers cycle on and off; crossing an active beam adds 40% alarm. The final vault needs every other piece first, takes seven seconds to crack, and adds a 25% security pulse. Take half the pieces, rounded up, to qualify for full loot value and a possible ghost bonus. Early exits receive half loot value and a reduced time bonus. Each alarm trip costs $250.

Run locally with `node serve.ts`, then open `http://localhost:8080`. Hand in with `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`. There are no runtime dependencies or network calls. Screenshot routes: `?shot=start|play|end&seed=3107`.
